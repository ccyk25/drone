GEMA hearing lab

This runs the same detector the phones use on real recordings: a flooded river, heavy rain, real screams, and a spoken "help" muffled as if through rubble (low-pass filtered to 600 Hz). Each scenario starts with 5 seconds of background so the noise floor adapts, exactly like a phone would. Recordings: Wikimedia Commons, credited in /sounds/ATTRIBUTION.md.

Camera eyes lab

Runs the same person detector the phones use (COCO-SSD lite, in the browser) on real flood and rescue photos, plus a drone's-eye view and a no-people satellite image. Red boxes = whole image; orange = tiled (image cut into 4 overlapping tiles so small, distant people get found). Photos: Wikimedia Commons, credited in /images/ATTRIBUTION.md.
